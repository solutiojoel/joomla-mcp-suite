Welcome to Good Shepherd Catholic Cemetery

Holy Cross is sacred ground where we honor the dignity of every life in faith, hope, and love. As the only Catholic diocesan cemetery in San Diego County, we provide compassionate care, prayerful support, and respectful burial services rooted in the teachings of the Church and the promise of eternal life in Christ. Our serene grounds offer a place for quiet reflection, remembrance, and trust in the Resurrection.
