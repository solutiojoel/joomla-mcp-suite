STREAM at St. Katherine of Siena brings Science, Technology, Religion, Engineering, Arts, and Math together in hands-on projects across all grade levels. Students visit the STREAM lab weekly, where lessons connect classroom learning to real-world problem solving rooted in our Catholic faith.
